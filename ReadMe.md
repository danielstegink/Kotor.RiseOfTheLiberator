# Rise of the Liberator

Game: KOTOR 1

Author: Dodrari Kaan

Version: 1.0.0

GitHub: https://github.com/danielstegink/Kotor.RiseOfTheLiberator



Within the world of Kotor, there are hints that the Mandalorians are stirring, that a new Mandalore is rising to claim the mantle after the fall of Mandalore the Ultimate. Now, with Rise of the Liberator, that mystery has a name and a face. Face the Mandalorians of Kotor as you haven't faced them before, with new types, new equipment, new maps, and a mighty foe waiting for you at the end of the galaxy!



This mod adds 2 new maps to the Rakata homeworld, where a new Mandalore and a small army of Mandalorians wait for you. Additionally, Mandalorian encounters throughout the game hint at the rise of this new Mandalore, transforming the Mandalorian experience into a proper sub-plot. Encounter new Mandalorian types, each with a unique armor that comes in both heltmetless and helmeted versions, and face the final boss to claim a very special weapon.
